# The World in One Day — Monday 23 November 2026

One picture that sums up a whole day of world news, drawn as a newspaper front page and entered in a make.fun bounty.

The front page leads with Dymer, where the death toll from the Russian drone strike on an apartment block reached 11 as the search of the rubble came to an end. Around it sit the day's other pictured stories: Kyiv's claim of the Benghazi drone-boat attack, the rising toll from the Cikande factory fire in Indonesia, the reported NATO assessment of a Russian nuclear-armed satellite, Jensen Huang's reinstatement as Nvidia CEO, Trump's warning to Iran, and Delta's holiday-travel meltdown. The artwork was laid out as an HTML newspaper front page and rendered into a 1024×1024 PNG; the small Python program beside it, `main.py`, simply copies that committed image to `/output/entry.png`.
