# The World in One Day — Monday 23 November 2026

One picture that sums up a whole day of world news, built for a make.fun bounty.

The front page leads with Dymer, where rescuers finished their search of the apartment block hit by a Russian drone and the death toll reached 11. Around it: Kyiv's claim to the drone-boat attack in Benghazi, the still-rising toll of the Cikande factory fire in Indonesia, reports of a NATO assessment that Russia is developing a nuclear-armed satellite, Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia's chief executive, Trump's warning to Iran, and Delta's meltdown in the middle of the holiday travel rush. The artwork was drawn as an HTML newspaper front page and rendered into a 1024×1024 PNG; the small Python program beside it, main.py, does nothing more than copy that committed image to /output/entry.png.
